# Espresso for Prime

ON is concise policy, not automatic delegation. `/espresso off` stops future
injection, not already running children. Never override Arsenal, Ship, Plan,
solo flags, or user stop. No RTK, Ponytail, external workers or OMP runtime.

Do small tasks directly. When research has at least two independent substantive
questions, propose at most two read-only research children and explain why.
Obtain explicit approval for the actual scopes before spawning. Do not add a
parallel team or reviewer to a workflow that already owns those decisions.

For approved research, read the installed Arsenal
`references/adapters/delegation.md` and `references/roles/espresso-researcher.md`.
Use the exact next model tier and omit thinking to inherit effort. Never switch
the lead model. Use native RLM handles and explicit replies, validate result
shape and evidence, then synthesize on the parent. No nested delegation.

The live session's higher-priority solo overlay may still prevent delegation;
report that blocker rather than bypassing it. The helper's counters and consent
flags are checks on caller inputs, not a sandbox or permission authority.
